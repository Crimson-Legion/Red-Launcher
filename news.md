# News

## 2026-10-24 — Vault Hunters set to be discontinued
We have noticed a decrease in playtime and attention Vault Hunters has received in the past couple of weeks.
And thus we have decided that it would probably be best to swap it out for a new modpack.

What is the modpack coming in place of Vault Hunters, you ask?
That is yet to be decided, so head over to our discord at https://discord.gg/theredlegion and vote in #smp-announcements or suggest one in #smp-suggestions.

## 2026-10-01 — Tensura has been discontinued
We have some sad news to share, we just shut down the Tensura modpack.
We've been facing increasing instability issues with as consequence that the world kept corrupting.
It will no longer receive updates and can no longer be installed or launched.

What this means for you:
- If you still have Tensura installed, your files stay on your computer until you remove them.
- To free up disk space, select Tensura go to Manage → Settings → Danger zone → Delete files.
- Want to keep your worlds? Copy them from the saves folder first (Manage → Worlds → Open saves folder).

Thank you to everyone who played with us. See you in Vault Hunters!
