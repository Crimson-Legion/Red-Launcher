# Privacy Policy for Red Launcher

**Last Updated:** August 6, 2026  
**Official Website:** https://red-legion.vercel.app  

This Privacy Policy explains how **Red Launcher** (operated jointly by **IronPathway7** [US], **Sinister_Red_** [EU], and **The Red Legion** team) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our custom Minecraft launcher and website.

### 1. Information We Collect
We collect minimal data necessary to authenticate your Minecraft account, keep the launcher functioning, process voluntary server support, and manage access to our SMP server.

* **Minecraft Account Data:** When you log in, we receive your publicly available Minecraft **Username** and **UUID** (Unique Identifier), as well as a temporary session token provided by Microsoft OAuth.
* **Technical & Diagnostic Information:**
  * Your IP address (when connecting to our server endpoints, Vercel web services, or SMP server).
  * Launcher logs, crash reports, operating system type, and Java version (used solely for debugging errors).
* **Payment Information (PayPal):** If you make a direct transfer/payment via PayPal, we may receive transaction identifiers, your PayPal email, or account name to confirm payment receipt. We **never** see or store your payment card or banking info.

### 2. Information We DO NOT Collect
* **Passwords:** We **NEVER** ask for, collect, process, or store your Microsoft or Minecraft password. All authentication is handled directly on Microsoft's official servers (`login.live.com`).

### 3. How We Use Your Information
We use the information collected to:
1. Authenticate your Minecraft session so you can log into the game.
2. Automatically download necessary mods, assets, and updates for the SMP server.
3. Diagnose bugs, launcher crashes, and performance issues.
4. Confirm and process PayPal transfers for server funding.
5. Enforce server bans and prevent cheating/ban evasion.

### 4. Third-Party Services
Our Launcher and website interact with third-party services:
* **Microsoft / Mojang:** Used for account authentication. ([Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/en-us/privacystatement))
* **PayPal:** Used for handling payments and server contributions. ([PayPal Privacy Statement](https://www.paypal.com/us/legalhub/privacy-full))
* **Vercel:** Hosts our web domain and assets (`red-legion.vercel.app`). ([Vercel Privacy Policy](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy))
* **Discord RPC (Optional):** Displays rich presence status (e.g., "Playing Red Legion SMP") on your Discord profile if enabled.

### 5. EU Privacy Rights (GDPR Compliance)
Because leadership and users include residents of the European Union, EU users have specific legal rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):
* **Legal Basis for Processing:** We process your data based on *Contractual Necessity* (to allow you to log in and play on the SMP) and *Legitimate Interest* (to maintain server security and prevent cheating).
* **Right to Access:** You may request a copy of any personal data we hold linked to your Minecraft UUID or IP address.
* **Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"):** You may request that we delete crash logs, server logs, or registered account profiles associated with you, provided it does not conflict with active ban-enforcement records.
* **Right to Object/Restrict:** You may object to data processing by discontinuing use of the Launcher and requesting data removal via Discord or email.

### 6. International Data Transfers
By using Red Launcher, you acknowledge that your information (e.g., IP address, UUID, crash logs) may be transferred, processed, and stored on servers located in both the **United States** and the **European Union**.

### 7. Children's Privacy
Red Launcher is designed for playing Minecraft and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain EU member states) without parental consent. Minecraft account permissions are managed via Microsoft Family settings.
